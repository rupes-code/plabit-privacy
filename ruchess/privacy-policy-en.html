RuChess Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · 한국어

Plabit (the "developer") values the privacy of users of RuChess (the "app"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information.

Game records, puzzle and lesson progress, and settings created in the app are stored only on your device and are never sent to any server. The chess engine (AI) also runs entirely on your device.

2. Data storage

All data is kept only in your device's local storage. The developer cannot access it.

  • Game records (moves, clock times, takeback and hint counts, results)
  • Solved puzzles and completed lessons
  • App settings (language, theme, board colors, sound)

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device.

If you have turned on Android backup (Google account backup) on your device, the Android system may include this data in your own Google account backup. That backup is governed by Google's policies, and the developer cannot access it.

3. Network use

The app does not use an internet connection. It does not request network permission and sends no data anywhere.

4. Sharing with third parties

The app shares no data with third parties. It contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or other external services.

5. Children's privacy

The app may be used by people of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information, no special measures for children's personal information are needed.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if ads or paid features are added), we will announce the changes through an app update or on this page.
