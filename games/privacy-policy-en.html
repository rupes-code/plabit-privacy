Plabit Games Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 26, 2026

This policy applies to all game apps published on Google Play by Plabit (the "Developer"), such as 2048, Flow Line, and others (the "App"). The Developer values your privacy and explains its practices below.

1. Information We Collect

The Developer does not directly collect any personally identifiable information. Your gameplay data (scores, records, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Developer's servers.

However, to serve ads, the third-party ad provider (Google) described in the "Advertising" section may automatically collect and use certain information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and IP address.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

This App displays ads through Google AdMob to support development. To deliver, measure, and personalize ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect and use:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • Device information (model, OS version, language), IP address, and approximate location
  • App usage and ad-interaction data

In regions where consent is required (EEA, UK, Switzerland, etc.), you can choose whether to allow personalized ads via an in-app consent screen; if you decline, non-personalized ads are shown. For details on how Google uses data, see:

  • How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Storage

Gameplay data is kept only in your device's local storage and is fully removed when you uninstall the App. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings.

4. Permissions

The App may use the following permissions, only for advertising and app functionality:

  • Internet/Network: used to load ads.
  • Vibrate: used for in-game haptic feedback.

5. Third Parties

The Developer does not sell or directly share your information with third parties. The App uses Google AdMob to show ads, and information collected in that process is handled under Google's policies.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for users aged 13 and older. The Developer does not direct the App to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

You can reset your advertising ID or disable personalized ads in your device settings, and where consent is required you can change your ad consent via the in-app consent screen.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will notify you through an app update or on this page.
